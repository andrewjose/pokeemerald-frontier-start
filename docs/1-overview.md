# Battle Frontier Start: overview for players

## What it is

In Pokémon Emerald, the Battle Frontier is the post-game area: seven facilities with their own rules (the Battle Tower, Factory, Dome, Palace, Arena, Pike and Pyramid). Getting there normally means finishing the whole story, then breeding and training a competitive team, which takes dozens of hours.

Battle Frontier Start skips all of that. A new game drops you at the Battle Frontier with a ready team, a PC full of more teams, and the tools to see what's going on in each battle. It's for people who want to play the Frontier itself.

## Starting a game

1. The title screen reads EMERALD with BATTLE FRONTIER underneath.
2. Choose NEW GAME. There's no Professor Birch speech: you pick boy or girl and a name right away.
3. A short guide explains the hack. You can reread it any time from OPTION > GUIDE.
4. Pick a **settings preset**:
   - **VANILLA**: Emerald's own settings, with this hack's extra features turned off.
   - **VANILLA+**: the hack's features on, FAST text. This is the default.
   - **MODERN**: everything on, plus INSTANT text, battle animations off, SET battle style (no free switch after a knockout, as in newer games) and your win streak kept if you quit without saving.
5. Pick a **fighting style**: Recommended, Offense, Stall, Speed Control, Trick Room, Rain, Sun or Sand. You get that team at level 50 and appear in the middle of the Battle Frontier.

You already have all eight badges, the National Pokédex, running shoes, a bag of useful items (berries, TMs, HMs, Master Balls, Rare Candies, vitamins, Max Repels) and every facility open.

## Your PC

The PC starts with 14 boxes of Pokémon:

- One box per fighting style (Offense, Stall, Balanced, Speed Control, Trick Room, Rain, Sun, Sand), each with the team at level 50 and again at level 100.
- The legendaries, at level 50 and at level 100.
- The Battle Tower fighters and sample teams from the "Conquer the Tower" guide, at both levels. They're shiny, with their Hidden Power types already set.

Every Pokémon in the PC has you as its original trainer, so it obeys you and counts as yours.

## In battle

- **L+R** opens an info screen showing every Pokémon on both sides: IVs, EVs, nature, stats, stat changes, ability, held item, HP, status and moves. Press A to switch to a damage view: for each of your moves, the range of damage it would do to the opponent you pick (SELECT picks another target). B goes back to the battle. Nothing about the battle changes while you look.
- **Damage preview:** when you highlight a move, the target's HP bar flickers between its current HP and what it would have left after the hit. The empty part is damage the move always does; the pale part depends on the random damage roll.
- At the **Battle Factory**, L+R also works on the screens where you rent and swap Pokémon.

L+R doesn't work in link battles or recorded battles.

## Changing Pokémon

Choose **EDIT** on a Pokémon in the party menu or the PC to change anything about it: species, nickname, level, nature, ability, gender, shininess, held item, Poké Ball, IVs, EVs, friendship, Hidden Power type, moves and PP Ups. L/R move to the next Pokémon, START saves and B leaves. EDIT isn't offered in the Battle Pyramid and Battle Pike, where you can't normally change your team.

The **move tutors' house** (Battle Frontier) teaches its moves for free and also has a move relearner and a move deleter.

## OPTION menu

Every change the hack makes to how the game plays can be switched back:

| Option | What it does |
|---|---|
| TEXT SPEED | Adds INSTANT (whole text box at once) |
| DMG PREVIEW | Damage preview on the HP bars |
| INFO START | Whether the L+R screen opens on the foe's side or yours |
| L+R INFO | The L+R info screen itself |
| EDIT MENU | EDIT in the party menu and PC |
| FREE TUTORS | OFF makes the Frontier tutors charge Battle Points, as in the original |
| FACTORY IVS | VANILLA brings back the original Battle Factory bug (opponent strength based on your Battle Tower streak instead of your Factory streak) |
| KEEP STREAK | Keep your win streak if you turn the game off mid-challenge without saving |
| GUIDE | Shows the overview again |

## Other things to know

- v1.47 and v1.48 change nothing you'll see in the game. It updates the code underneath to the latest version of the community project the hack is built on, and builds the game with a newer compiler, the same way every time, so each version is checked automatically before it's released.

- R + START on the map opens a debug menu (warping, items, flags and more).
- The hack needs a new game. Loading an old Emerald save won't give you the Frontier start.
